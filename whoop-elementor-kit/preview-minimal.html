About

In the studio

I paint the characters we grew up with — loud, and full of heart.

I'm a contemporary pop artist. My work takes familiar, playful characters and reimagines them in thick paint, scribbled hearts and colour that won't sit still.

Every canvas is an original — hand-painted, signed, and made to make someone smile.
