Studio Notes

Street art, colour theory, and painting out loud

By Whatshername  ·  13 August 2026  ·  6 min read

Featured Image

There's a myth that good taste means restraint. I've never believed it. The work that moves me is the work that isn't afraid to be too much — too bright, too fast, too honest.

This piece started, like most of them, with a feeling I couldn't name. So I reached for the loudest colour on the table and started making marks until the feeling had a shape.

"Colour is the place where our brain and the universe meet."
— a line I keep above the easel

Colour as a language

Every hue carries a mood. Magenta shouts, cyan hums, yellow laughs. When I put them next to each other, I'm really writing a sentence — the composition is just grammar.

In the Studio

By the end, the painting knows more than I did when I started. That's the whole point of making it.
