Meet the Artist

Hi, I'm Whatshername — I make loud, feeling-first art.

I'm a contemporary painter working in acrylic and mixed media. My work is about big feelings and the colours that come with them — joy, noise, restlessness and calm.

Based in London, I show work in galleries, paint murals, and make limited prints so the art can live in more homes.

How I Work

What guides the work

❤

Feeling first

Every piece starts as an emotion, not a plan. The colour follows the feeling.

🎨

Colour without apology

I use the loudest colours I can find and let them argue with each other.

🤝

Made to be lived with

Art shouldn't sit in storage. I make work — and prints — meant for real walls.
